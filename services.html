    Services FAQ

    How do deposits work with PayPal?

    One-time packages are paid through PayPal at checkout. Monthly packages are not included in that charge; Desanya Studio arranges the monthly billing with you after checkout. If the cart has only monthly packages, PayPal charges a $1 deposit so the order can be placed.

    How do revisions work?

    Each package lists how many revision rounds are included. A round is a set of changes on the draft you were sent. An extra round is $40. Nothing goes live until you approve it.

    How long does the work take?

    Each package lists its delivery time in business days, Monday through Friday. Rush delivery, under half that time, is 35% more and can be added in the cart.

    Do you work remote or local?

    There is one public price list. Desanya Studio is based in Niota, TN, serves Knoxville and the rest of East Tennessee, and works with remote clients anywhere. Intro calls can be remote or local. An in-person kickoff, or photos shot on site, can be added for Knoxville and East Tennessee clients.

    What are your hours?

    Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM ET. Messages after hours are answered the next business day.

    How long until SEO shows results?

    There is no guaranteed ranking. Search changes often take 1 to 3 months, and they depend on the site, the competition, and Google.
