    Meet our cozy Thanksgiving AI crew

    November 17, 2026 · Desanya Studio

    Same inbox, same deadlines, cozier sweaters. Behind Desanya Studio there's a small team of 10 AI helpers. They triage email, watch the bills, plan the content, and keep this website running. Last month they wore Halloween costumes. This month they traded the bats and witch hats for scarves, pie and autumn leaves.

    The look is the same crew in the same neon pink, plum and navy, just warmed up with candlelight and a little gold. Every helper keeps its pink bow and its job prop, so you can still tell who's who at a glance.

    One thing doesn't change, whatever the season: every helper drafts and prepares, and nothing is sent, spent, or published without Desanya's approval.

    The crew, all cozied up

    • Harvest Boo-tique Studio

      Desanya’s Studio

      Maintains desanya.tech and ships every site change as a reviewed update.

    • Harvest Mail Ghost

      Gmail Overseer

      Triages the inbox, labels every thread, and drafts replies for review.

    • Client Stew Pot

      Client Pipeline

      Finds local leads, tracks every client from first contact to finished project, and drafts follow-ups and review requests.

    • Bill Watcher of the Harvest

      Bill Watcher

      Keeps a short list of upcoming, past-due, and failed bills so nothing slips.

    • Cozy Cast Studio

      Content Studio

      Plans the weekly content calendar and drafts posts, scripts, and blog articles.

    • Harvest Rocket Launch

      Business Launch

      Owns the launch plan, offer packages, and the LLC checklist and deadlines.

    • Cornucopia Treasure Scout

      Side Income Scout

      Researches free-to-start side income ideas and delivers a short ranked list each week.

    • Pie-nance Desk

      Finance Desk

      Keeps the books ready each month with invoice drafts, categorized expenses, and a money checklist.

    • Pumpkin Pie Morning Planner

      Day Planner

      Builds a weekday morning plan from the calendar, with conflicts and top priorities.

    • Desanya After Dark: Harvest Hour

      Grok Bot · Desanya After Dark

      The coordinator that routes requests to the right assistant and tracks every handoff.

    Why a new look for Thanksgiving?

    The crew runs on one shared style with one prop per job. A seasonal look keeps all of that and changes only the details: the laptop, the mailbag and the ledger stay put, and the pumpkins, pie and leaves tell you what time of year it is. It's the same brand, just in a warmer sweater.

    Your brand can get cozy too

    These are AI customization avatars, made for our own assistants. Desanya Studio can make a set like this for your brand too: your mascot, logo character or team avatars, in your colors, with a seasonal look when you want one.
