    Meet our cozy Thanksgiving AI crew

    November 17, 2026 · Desanya Studio

    Same inbox, same deadlines, cozier sweaters. Behind Desanya Studio there's a small team of 10 AI helpers. They triage email, watch the bills, plan the content, and keep this website running. Last month they wore Halloween costumes. This month they've packed those away and turned into a Thanksgiving harvest: a pumpkin, a turkey, a cornucopia, an acorn, an ear of corn, a maple leaf, a squirrel, a pumpkin pie, a hedgehog and a Highland calf.

    The look is all golden afternoon light, candlelight and harvest colors: orange, gold, cranberry and cream, with our signature pink and plum. Every helper keeps its pink bow and its job prop, so you can still tell who's who at a glance.

    One thing doesn't change, whatever the season: every helper drafts and prepares, and nothing is sent, spent, or published without Desanya's approval.

    The crew, all cozied up

    • Pumpkin Patch Studio

      Desanya’s Studio

      Maintains desanya.tech and ships every site change as a reviewed update.

    • Gobble Gobble Mail

      Gmail Overseer

      Triages the inbox, labels every thread, and drafts replies for review.

    • Cornucopia of Clients

      Client Pipeline

      Finds local leads, tracks every client from first contact to finished project, and drafts follow-ups and review requests.

    • Acorn Bill Lookout

      Bill Watcher

      Keeps a short list of upcoming, past-due, and failed bills so nothing slips.

    • Corn on the Mic

      Content Studio

      Plans the weekly content calendar and drafts posts, scripts, and blog articles.

    • Maple Leaf Liftoff

      Business Launch

      Owns the launch plan, offer packages, and the LLC checklist and deadlines.

    • Squirrel Stash Scout

      Side Income Scout

      Researches free-to-start side income ideas and delivers a short ranked list each week.

    • Pie-nance Desk

      Finance Desk

      Keeps the books ready each month with invoice drafts, categorized expenses, and a money checklist.

    • Hedgie's Harvest Planner

      Day Planner

      Builds a weekday morning plan from the calendar, with conflicts and top priorities.

    • Desanya After Dark: Highland Harvest Hour

      Grok Bot · Desanya After Dark

      The coordinator that routes requests to the right assistant and tracks every handoff.

    Why a new look for Thanksgiving?

    The crew runs on one shared style with one prop per job. For Thanksgiving, each helper became a harvest character, and the props stayed put: the laptop, the mail, the magnifying glass, the microphone, the rocket, the lantern, the ledger and the latte. The pink bows tie it all together. It's the same brand crew, just gathered around the harvest table.
