    Meet our spooky AI crew

    October 30, 2026 · Desanya Studio

    Same inbox, same deadlines, better outfits. Behind Desanya Studio there's a small team of 10 AI helpers. They triage email, watch the bills, plan the content, and keep this website running. This Halloween they're all in costume.

    We picked one of 2026's most popular Halloween looks: spooky-cute, with chubby ghosts, round pumpkins and pastel-goth bows. Then we turned it neon pink, plum and navy to match the studio. We call it Neon Pinkoween. It's cute and never gory.

    One thing doesn't change in costume: every helper drafts and prepares, and nothing is sent, spent, or published without Desanya's approval.

    Why give AI helpers a Halloween look?

    The crew has a real system behind it: one shared style and one prop per job, so you can tell what each helper does at a glance. A seasonal costume is the same idea. The brand stays recognizable, it just dresses up for a week.

    How brands do Halloween

    We weren't the first to put a mascot in costume. A few examples we looked at:

    • Duolingo's owl, Duo, has a whole costume closet and a dedicated costume designer, according to Adweek. In 2025, fans dressed up as Duo so often that the CEO brought it up on an earnings call.
    • Duolingo's chatbot characters got their own Halloween looks and a trick-or-treating chat adventure.
    • Prime Gaming gave its profile pictures and banners on X, YouTube and Twitch a Halloween treatment.
    • AI assistants became costumes themselves in 2025, with people dressing up as their favorite chatbot, as Business Insider reported.
    • Small businesses often treat it as putting the logo in costume: keep it recognizable, add one seasonal touch, and check it still reads at profile-picture size.

    What these examples share: the costume fits the character, the brand stays easy to spot, and it's all in fun.
