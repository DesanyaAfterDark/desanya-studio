    Sherman’s Shih Tzu Puppies ↗ (opens in a new tab)

    A family who raises AKC and CKC Shih Tzus in Niota, East Tennessee, and welcomes families who drive in from all over the US.

    Before

    Weekends at the Sweetwater Flea Market meant long days and thin margins. After booth fees, gas, food, and shots, there was barely enough left to care for the dogs. Then COVID closed the market for good. It could have been the end.

    What we built

    Desanya Studio’s first build was a real website, and a Google Business Profile to go with it.

    After

    2,186
    Google Business Profile interactions, May–Oct 2026

    Today, families drive in from all over the US to meet the dogs in person. They don’t ship. Google recorded 2,186 Google Business Profile interactions, May–Oct 2026 — calls, messages, directions, and website clicks. The business grew enough to build the dogs a two-story home of their own.

    Ongoing care

    Desanya fully manages the website and the verified Google Business Profile, including regular updates about dogs who are ready for their families.

    Your business can have that same kind of care: a website, a Google Business Profile, and Desanya Studio to manage both.

    Case study · Client logos

    NV Home Designs

    A calm, premium logo for a kitchen & bath design and project-management studio in Northern California.

    What we did

    • Brand palette: black, white and grey, neutral like the finishes.
    • Logo design: a thin-line roof and chimney, a clean sans-serif wordmark and the owner’s handwritten signature.
    • Social profile assets: the logo set up for a light Facebook cover and a dark profile picture.

    Why it works

    One thin line draws the roof and chimney, so the mark reads “home” at a glance and feels like a designer’s sketch. The open sans-serif stays easy to read on a cover photo, a sign or a phone, and the signature puts a real person behind the work. Kept to black, white and grey, it sits as easily on a bright kitchen photo as on a dark wall.

    Studio identity

    Brand evolution

    Before Desanya Studio had a clear identity, its look had been built up from a series of 2018 freelance commissions: a green tech “D” logo, an iTi site icon and banner, a smoke-style logo intro, and gaming art. Each piece was made by a different freelance designer for a different purpose. Together they read as several brands rather than one.

    The studio kept what that work had taught and rebuilt the system from scratch. The new set uses one navy and plum canvas and the pink-and-gold dragonfly D logo. That logo carries through the site icon, social banners, the YouTube end screen, and the seven-second logo sting. The gaming After Dark mark stays separate and was tidied for stream use only.

    The original pieces are earlier 2018 freelance work. The redesigns are Desanya Studio’s.
