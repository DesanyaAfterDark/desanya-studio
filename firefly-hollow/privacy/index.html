    Firefly Hollow Privacy Policy

    Effective: [date of v1.0 release]
    Developer: Desanya Studio
    Contact: desanyaafterdark@gmail.com

    1. Summary

    Firefly Hollow has no accounts, no ads and no chat. We use two service providers: Google Firebase Analytics (to understand how the game is played) and RevenueCat (to process and restore in-app purchases made through Google Play). We do not sell your data.

    2. Age check

    On first launch we ask for a birth year. We store only the year and an age band on your device. If you are under 13, or don't answer, analytics stays off and no analytics data leaves the device.

    3. Analytics (players 13 and older)

    With analytics on, Firebase collects a random app-instance ID, game events (for example levels started and completed, screens viewed, session length), purchase events (product, price), device model, OS version, app version, language, and an approximate location (country/region) derived from a masked IP address. We do not collect the Android Advertising ID, set a user ID, or link analytics to Google Ads. You can turn analytics off in Settings. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy (opens in a new tab)

    4. Purchases

    Purchases are optional and go through Google Play. Google handles your payment details; we never see them. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history (product, price, currency, date, and the Google Play purchase token) so we can deliver and restore what you bought. Every purchase is behind a grown-up check. RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy (opens in a new tab)

    5. Stored on your device

    Game progress, settings, birth year and age band. Uninstalling or resetting progress deletes them.

    6. Sharing

    Only with the service providers above, acting for us, and when required by law. No data is sold or used for advertising.

    7. Retention and deletion

    Analytics data is kept for 2 months (the GA4 retention setting), then deleted. Purchase records are kept as long as needed for restores, refunds and tax law. To delete your data, email desanyaafterdark@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data"; we'll delete your RevenueCat customer record and analytics data within 30 days.

    8. Children

    Firefly Hollow is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; players who give an under-13 birth year are not tracked. A parent who thinks we hold a child's data can email us for deletion.

    9. Security

    Data sent to our providers is encrypted in transit (TLS).

    10. Changes

    We'll update this page and the effective date, and note changes in the app's release notes.
