    Firefly Hollow Privacy Policy

    App: Firefly Hollow
    Bundle id: com.desanya.fireflyhollow
    Developer: Desanya Studio
    Contact: desanyaafterdark@gmail.com
    Effective date: 31 October 2026

    This is the policy for the Firefly Hollow mobile game. It is the text to host at a public URL before the App Store and Google Play submissions.

    What we collect

    We do not collect personal data. We do not run analytics. We do not use an advertising SDK. We do not sell data, share data with brokers, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

    The game does not ask you to create an account. It does not read your contacts, photos, microphone, precise location, or advertising id.

    What stays on your device

    Progress is stored on the device only: the level you have reached, coins, renovation stars, lives, settings, and a birth year you type in so the game can tell a player under 13 from an older player. That birth year is not sent to Desanya Studio. Resetting progress on the device deletes the local save. Uninstalling the app deletes it too.

    Players under 13 are not tracked. Analytics collection is off for every player in this version. A player who has not answered the birth-year question is treated the same way.

    Purchases

    This version does not sell anything. The Restore Purchases button does not contact a store and does not restore a charge, because purchases are off. Stars cannot be bought. If a later version turns purchases on, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the store forms will be answered again.

    Children

    Firefly Hollow is a cozy game that children can play. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. We do not show ads. We do not sell in-game items in this version.

    Links

    A privacy note inside the game can open a page about children's privacy. That link waits for a grown-up to answer a short math question. The game itself does not send your save with that link.

    Changes

    If this policy changes, the new date will be at the top of this page, and the in-game note will point at the updated page.

    Contact

    Questions about this policy: desanyaafterdark@gmail.com
